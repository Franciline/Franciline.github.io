KaLC Lab - University of Tsukuba, Ibaraki, Japan

Studied emotion-cause extraction from patient narratives, with potential applications in understanding and monitoring patients’ experiences during treatment. The work also aimed to establish a standardized dataset and evaluation framework for research.

  • Built a privacy-aware collection and preprocessing pipeline for 18 breast cancer patient blogs and 2,500+ posts across two platforms, following scraping policies.
  • Iteratively refined emotion-cause annotation guidelines across three versions and curated a 147-pair reference set.

  • Developed an automated evaluation framework combining ROUGE-L, sentence-embedding cosine similarity, and Hungarian matching.
  • Conducted systematic error analysis to compare model performance and identify extraction limitations.
